========================================
  TradeSphere - Folder Rename Commands
========================================

To complete the rebranding, rename the folder from "emporia-main" to "tradesphere"

------------------------
WINDOWS (CMD)
------------------------
cd c:\Github-all-repos
rename emporia-main tradesphere

------------------------
WINDOWS (PowerShell)
------------------------
Rename-Item -Path "c:\Github-all-repos\emporia-main" -NewName "tradesphere"

------------------------
After Renaming
------------------------
New path will be:
c:\Github-all-repos\tradesphere\

Open the project in your IDE from this new location.
